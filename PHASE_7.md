### Executive Summary

Phase 7 transforms the **MMJ-DOCS Market Intelligence Platform** from an observational dashboard and alerting tool into an **evidence-driven market research, hypothesis testing, and decision-support optimization engine**.

Every metric, pattern, and geographic opportunity score generated by this engine is strictly grounded in verifiable database records, documented statistical methodologies, and auditable citation trails. Untested hypotheses are strictly isolated from verified facts, simulations carry a mathematically guaranteed **Zero-Production-Mutation** constraint, and an automated background scanner actively monitors and flags stale or invalidated findings as new market data arrives.

### 1. Initial Gap Analysis & Starting State (Audit)

Prior to Phase 7, the platform contained functioning Phase 1–6 infrastructure:
- **Phase 1–3**: Core database models (`Entity`, `EntityLocation`, `CompetitorPrice`, `PriceHistory`, `Dispensary`, `StateMarketMetric`), Alembic migrations, scraping workers (`base_collector`, Florida OMMU, Weedmaps, Leafly), and REST API layer.
- **Phase 4**: NLP-based natural language Q&A (`/api/market-intelligence/ai/ask`) and basic forecasting (`HoltWintersEngine`).
- **Phase 5**: Real-time change intelligence engine (`AuditLog`, anomaly detection, alerting channels).
- **Phase 6**: Enterprise operational readiness, RBAC (`Viewer`, `Editor`, `Admin`), Redis distributed caching, Prometheus metrics, and automated backup/restore scripts.

**Gaps Identified in Phase 1–6 (Addressed in Phase 7):**
1. *Absence of Research Structure*: No mechanism existed to group inquiries into projects, formulate falsifiable hypotheses, attach verifiable evidence, or govern findings through a formal peer-review lifecycle.
2. *Lack of Multi-Level Geographic Explainability*: Geographic opportunity scoring was aggregated solely at the state level with static coefficients, lacking factor contribution waterfalls, model versioning, or County/ZIP drill-downs.
3. *Ad-Hoc Competitive Observations*: Competitive pricing changes were logged linearly; the system could not differentiate sustained strategic pricing cuts from temporary promotional dips, nor provide a mechanism to dismiss false positives.
4. *Absence of Reusable Statistical Layer*: No validated statistical library existed for computing Student's t confidence intervals, Pearson/Spearman correlations with p-values, OLS time-series decomposition, or Pettitt non-parametric change-point detection with sample sufficiency guards.
5. *Uncoupled What-If Modeling*: Management had no capability to test alternative business assumptions (e.g., entering a new state, adjusting price elasticity, scaling physician capacity) without risking corruption of production operational metrics.
6. *Service Non-Equivalence in Pricing*: Pricing comparisons compared initial consultation and renewal certifications indiscriminately without tracking package inclusions, durations, or equivalence warnings.
7. *AI Hallucination & Citation Grounding*: The existing AI copilot returned text summaries without structured citations, without clear separation of facts vs. estimates vs. hypotheses, and without disclosing data gaps.

### 3. Service Layer Architecture

The Phase 7 service layer is located under `backend/app/services/`:

#### 1. `ResearchWorkspaceService` (`app/services/research_workspace_service.py`)
- **Lifecycle State Machine**: Validates transitions across all 7 statuses (`DRAFT`, `UNDER_INVESTIGATION`, `EVIDENCE_REVIEWED`, `VALIDATED`, `NEEDS_REASSESSMENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `REJECTED`). Every transition generates an immutable `ResearchReviewHistory` record.
- **Automated Reassessment Scanner**: Compares observation dates against fresh database entries in `PriceHistory` and `StateMarketMetric`. If market prices drift $>15\%$ or newer records exist, flags finding status to `NEEDS_REASSESSMENT` without destroying historical evidence.
- **Dossier Export**: Serializes projects, active hypotheses, validated findings, contradictory evidence, and source citations into structured Markdown dossiers and JSON schemas.

#### 2. `StatisticalAnalysisService` (`app/services/statistical_analysis_service.py`)
- **Descriptive & Inferential Statistics**:
  - Sample mean, median, sample variance ($n-1$), standard deviation, skewness (Fisher-Pearson), and kurtosis.
  - Student's t distribution confidence intervals at 95% and 99% levels:
    $$\bar{x} \pm t_{\alpha/2, n-1} \cdot \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$$
- **Correlation Engine**:
  - Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient $r$ with two-tailed t-distribution p-value.
  - Spearman's rank correlation coefficient $\rho$ handling ties via fractional ranking.
- **Time-Series Decomposition**:
  - Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) linear slope, intercept, $R^2$, and annualized trend percentage.
- **Change-Point Detection**:
  - Pettitt's non-parametric rank-based test for abrupt shifts in series mean. Integrated small-sample exact critical threshold ($K \ge \lfloor 0.35 \cdot \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \rfloor$) and asymptotic $p$-value calculation:
    $$p \approx 2 \exp\left(-\frac{6 K^2}{n^3 + n^2}\right)$$
- **Comparative Testing**:
  - Welch's two-sample t-test (unequal variances) with Welch-Satterthwaite degrees of freedom.
- **Sensitivity Perturbation**:
  - Multi-variable perturbation matrices ($\pm 10\%, \pm 20\%$) assessing response elasticity.

#### 3. `CompetitivePatternService` (`app/services/competitive_pattern_service.py`)
- Analyzes longitudinal snapshots in `PriceHistory` and `CompetitorPrice` over a configurable horizon (7 to 365 days).
- Distinguishes sustained trends ($\ge 3$ consecutive price points lower by $>5\%$) from transient promotional surges.
- False-Positive Dismissal Workflow: Allows analysts to dismiss anomalies with recorded rationale (`dismiss_pattern`) and restore previously dismissed findings (`restore_pattern`).
- Transparently alerts analysts: *“Advertised price represents public surface-web positioning and does not guarantee realized transaction price.”*

#### 4. `AdvancedGeographicOpportunityService` (`app/services/advanced_geographic_opportunity_service.py`)
- Multi-level scoring support (`STATE`, `COUNTY`, `ZIP`).
- Evaluates 6 core components:
  1. *Patient Population & Growth* ($w=0.25$)
  2. *Dispensary Density & Proximity* ($w=0.20$)
  3. *Physician Coverage / Deficit* ($w=0.20$)
  4. *Competitive Pricing Margin Room* ($w=0.15$)
  5. *Regulatory & Telehealth Environment* ($w=0.10$)
  6. *Data Coverage & Observation Freshness* ($w=0.10$)
- Outputs a normalized 0–100 score accompanied by an exact **Factor Contribution Waterfall** showing point contributions from each factor.
- Model Versioning & What-If Simulation: Compares multiple states or regions under alternative weight configurations without mutating the active scoring model.
- Automatically saves historical scoring snapshots in `geographic_score_snapshots`.

#### 5. `ScenarioComparisonService` (`app/services/scenario_comparison_service.py`)
- **Zero-Mutation Guarantee**: All simulation definitions, baseline inputs, candidate inputs, and results are serialized exclusively into `scenario_definitions`. The production database remains unaltered.
- **Probabilistic 3-Case Projections**:
  - *Downside Case*: $-25\%$ patient conversion, $+15\%$ operational expenses.
  - *Expected Base Case*: Calibrated market conversion, regional price elasticity ($\beta = -1.2$).
  - *Best Case*: $+25\%$ conversion, $-10\%$ overhead efficiency, capped at physician capacity ceiling.
- **Multi-Dimensional Sensitivity Matrix**: Generates a 2D matrix crossing candidate price test points ($\pm 10\%$) with conversion shifts ($\pm 20\%$) to expose margin break-even points.

#### 6. `PricingResearchService` (`app/services/pricing_research_service.py`)
- Evaluates Initial Consultation vs. Renewal spreads across competitors and states.
- Emits **Service Non-Equivalence Warnings**: Flags comparisons where consultation length, physician tier, or included medical records differ between providers.
- Identifies **Market Pricing Gaps**: Computes the dollar difference between MMJ-DOCS rates and competitor market medians, categorizing opportunities as `PREMIUM_WINDOW`, `COMPETITIVE_DISCOUNT_RISK`, or `PARITY`.

#### 7. `EvidenceGroundedAiResearchService` (`app/services/evidence_grounded_ai_research_service.py`)
- Natural-language market research copilot executing deterministic SQL aggregations and analytical service calls.
- Enforces strict five-fold ontological separation:
  1. **`facts`**: Directly verified database records.
  2. **`calculations`**: Quantitative metrics computed by the analytical layer.
  3. **`estimates`**: Modeled proxies and scenario assumptions.
  4. **`hypotheses`**: Strategic inferences under evaluation.
  5. **`recommendations`**: Concrete next investigations.
- Returns audited citation objects referencing `source_table`, `record_id`, `observation_date`, and `value`.
- Discloses explicit `evidence_gaps` and `limitations` when data coverage is insufficient.

### 7. Security & Compliance Verification

1. **Zero-PHI Compliance Guard**:
   - Scrapers, analytics, and AI queries operate exclusively on public surface-web data (`CompetitorPrice`, `PriceHistory`, `Dispensary`, `StateMarketMetric`).
   - No patient clinical notes, diagnoses, medical records, or personal identifiers are ingested or queried.
2. **Surface-Web Only Restriction**:
   - Dark Web collection is strictly prohibited in architecture and code.
   - All collectors adhere to robots directives, standard HTTP user agents, and exponential backoff.
3. **Advertised vs. Realized Price Transparency**:
   - The UI and API explicitly disclose that advertised competitor pricing reflects public marketing positioning, not confidential transactional prices.
4. **Zero-Mutation Guarantee**:
   - Scenario simulations are strictly segregated into `scenario_definitions` and never alter live pricing, physician staffing, or operational records.
